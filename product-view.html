Product Description

Precision engineered stainless steel ball valve for dependable isolation in industrial fluid systems.

Designed for water, process fluid and utility applications, this full-bore valve combines a corrosion-resistant body with PTFE seats. The lockable handle supports controlled operation and straightforward maintenance. Our engineering team can help confirm suitability for your operating conditions.

Features

  • Stainless steel body and stem
  • Full bore design for low pressure drop
  • Lockable quarter-turn lever handle
  • Serviceable PTFE seats and seals
